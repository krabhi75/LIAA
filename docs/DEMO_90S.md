# 90-second submission video

## Fastest path (recommended)

1. Open **https://liaa-ebon.vercel.app/reel** (fullscreen `F11`).
2. Press **R** to restart from 0:00.
3. Screen-record for **90 seconds** (Windows: `Win+G` → Capture, or OBS).
4. Optional: read the voiceover below while recording (or add voice in CapCut later).

Controls: **Space** = pause · **R** = restart.

---

**(0–8s)**

**(8–16s)**

**(16–26s)**  
On Agora we run Deepgram for Hindi speech recognition, OpenAI GPT-4o-mini for reasoning, and ElevenLabs for speech. Agora owns the live channel and barge-in.

**(26–36s)**  
On the live desk, start a conversation, speak Hindi or Hinglish, interrupt mid-sentence — tools update on screen in realtime.

**(36–46s)**  
The field CRM holds farmer profiles, call timelines, and playable call recordings — structured from natural speech.

**(46–56s)**  
Outbound from CRM or Agora campaigns, and inbound on our Indian DID — name, then district, then how we can help. The call stays connected.

**(56–65s)**  
After location, live weather from Open-Meteo is spoken naturally and stored on the farmer profile. Never invented.

**(65–74s)**  
When confidence is low, KrishiSaathi creates an expert case with full context — escalation is success, not failure.

**(74–82s)**  
Architecture is Agora-central: voice on Conversational AI, Next.js for tokens, MCP tools, and Neon CRM.

**(82–90s)**  
Live at liaa-ebon.vercel.app — KrishiSaathi, voice-first agri support for Bharat. GitHub: krabhi75/LIAA.
